# Linking upgrades

Every remote upgrade stores its own link to one network endpoint. All eight upgrade types use the same method and work with Applied Energistics 2, Refined Storage, or Tom's Simple Storage. On NeoForge 1.21.1 with SCC 1.5.3 or newer installed, they can also link to Super Cool Calculator; there are no separate network-specific items.

1. Hold the remote upgrade.
2. Shift-right-click a compatible block: an ME Wireless Access Point for AE2, a Controller for Refined Storage, a Storage Terminal for Tom's Simple Storage, or an SCC Network Antenna on NeoForge 1.21.1.
3. Put the linked upgrade in a backpack upgrade slot and open its tab.

To change networks, link the upgrade to another compatible endpoint. To remove the link while keeping its settings, craft the upgrade by itself. These actions require player interaction permission and network access. The addon follows the network's own availability, wireless range, and permission rules; it does not create wireless access.

An upgrade with items or fluids pending recovery cannot be relinked or unlinked until they are recovered.
